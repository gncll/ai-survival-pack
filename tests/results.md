## 1. The Golden Rule: Temperature Control

The biggest enemy of non-refrigerated food is **room temperature**, which allows bacteria to multiply rapidly.

* **Keep it Cool (If Possible):** If you have a cool, dark pantry, that's better than a hot, sunny spot.
* **Use the Coldest Spots:** Store items in the coolest, darkest cabinets or closets you have.
* **Avoid Direct Sunlight:** Never store food near windows or under lights, as this will heat it up quickly.

---
